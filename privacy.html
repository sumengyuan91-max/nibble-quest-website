A little introduction

This policy explains how the Nibble Quest team handles information in connection with this website and the current Nibble Quest game experience. For privacy questions or requests, write to NibbleQuest@outlook.com.

This website has no player account, advertising scripts, analytics scripts or newsletter signup. You can browse, watch the video and try the little puzzle without entering personal information.

02

What information is involved?

  • Website requests. The hosting provider receives the information needed to deliver pages, images and video, such as an IP address, requested URL, browser information and request time. The live website is hosted with GitHub Pages; its handling of service information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement ↗.
  • Your device. The website stores your animation preference in your browser. The little puzzle runs in memory; its moves are not sent to a game server.
  • Game progress. The current game stores progress, unlocked items, favorites and settings on the device. This website does not connect to or read that save file.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Please avoid including passwords, payment-card numbers or other sensitive information.
03

How information is used

We use support information to answer your request, investigate reported bugs and improve the experience. Technical request information helps the hosting provider deliver and protect the website. Your animation preference is used only to remember your choice on this browser.

There is no advertising or sale of personal information through this website. Emailing support does not subscribe you to marketing messages.

04

Cookies & local storage

The website uses one local-storage entry, nq-site-motion, to remember whether you paused animations. It does not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking scripts. You can remove this entry by clearing this website’s browser data. Private browsing or browser restrictions may prevent the preference from being saved.

Clearing a mobile game’s app data or uninstalling it can remove locally stored progress. This website cannot restore a save file that is no longer on your device.

05

Services & links

Website hosting and email delivery involve their service providers. Support email is received through Outlook; see Microsoft’s Privacy Statement ↗. We may share the minimum information necessary with providers helping us handle a request, or where a valid legal obligation requires disclosure.

App Store, Google Play, publisher-policy and other external links lead to services with their own policies. Following a link sends a request to that service. The website does not automatically sign you into a store or send it your game progress.

We will update this page before introducing a new data-collection feature, such as account-based cloud saves, advertising or analytics. Store privacy disclosures should describe the actual released game version.

06

Retention & security

We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and any follow-up, unless a longer period is required for a legal or security reason. You may ask us to delete information you provided. Hosting and email providers apply their own retention practices to service records.

We limit the information requested for support and use HTTPS for the website. No internet or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

07

Your choices & requests

You can browse without contacting us, pause animations, clear browser storage or stop using the site. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict certain personal information.

To make a request, email NibbleQuest@outlook.com with “Privacy request” in the subject. Tell us what you would like to do. We may need limited information to verify a request and protect another person’s data.

08

Younger players

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website. There is no account or personal-information form in the browser puzzle. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us personal information can contact us to request its removal.

09

Updates & contact

If our practices change, we will update this page and its date. Material changes will be highlighted on the website where appropriate.

Privacy contact: NibbleQuest@outlook.com
